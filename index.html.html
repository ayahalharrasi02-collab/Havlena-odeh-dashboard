1 How to Use the Preset Switcher & Prepare Your Excel File

Use the 1-Click Homework Dataset Switcher at the top of the dashboard to toggle between HW2 Question 1 (Gas-Cap Reservoir), HW2 Question 2 (Solution-Gas Reservoir), or your own uploaded Excel file. Click "Download Active Dataset as Excel (.xlsx)" to export a clean 2-sheet workbook:

Sheet 1: Production_PVT_Data

Must contain a header row with the following columns (order flexible):

  • P : Average reservoir pressure (psia)
  • Rs : Solution GOR (SCF/STB)
  • Bo : Oil formation volume factor (rb/STB)
  • Bg : Gas FVF (cuft/SCF or rb/STB)
  • Np : Cumulative oil produced (MMSTB or STB)
  • Gp : Cumulative gas produced (MMMSCF, MMSCF, or SCF)

Sheet 2: Initial_Parameters

Two-column key-value table (Property | Value):

  • pi : Initial reservoir pressure (psia)
  • Boi : Initial oil FVF (rb/STB)
  • Bgi : Initial gas FVF (same unit as Bg)
  • Rsi : Initial solution GOR (SCF/STB)
  • Swc : Connate water saturation (fraction)
  • Cw : Water compressibility (psi⁻¹)
  • Cf : Formation compressibility (psi⁻¹)
  • phi : Porosity (fraction, optional)

2 Havlena–Odeh Straight-Line Equations & Unit Conversions

The general material balance equation for a reservoir with an initial gas cap ($m$) and formation/connate water expansion ($E_{fw}$) is expressed by Havlena and Odeh (1963) as:

F = N · [ Eo + m · Eg + (1 + m) · Efw ]
where:
• Underground Withdrawal:   F   = Np [ Bo + (Rp − Rs) Bg ]    (with Rp = Gp / Np in SCF/STB)
• Oil & Gas Expansion:     Eo  = (Bo − Boi) + (Rsi − Rs) Bg
• Gas-Cap Expansion:       Eg  = Boi ( Bg / Bgi − 1 )
• Pore & Water Expansion:   Efw = Boi [ (Cw Swc + Cf) / (1 − Swc) ] (pi − p)
• Unit Conversions: 1 bbl = 5.614583 cuft ⇒ Bg [rb/SCF] = Bg [cuft/SCF] / 5.614583; 1 MMMSCF = 109 SCF

Dividing both sides by $(E_o + E_{fw})$ yields a straight line $Y = a + b X$:

Y = F / (Eo + Efw) = N + (m · N) · [ (Eg + Efw) / (Eo + Efw) ]
  • Y-Intercept ($a$): Stock-Tank Original Oil in Place, $N$ (MMSTB).
  • Slope ($b$): Product $m \cdot N$, so the gas-cap ratio is $m = b / a$.
  • Initial Gas-Cap Gas in Place ($G$): Computed from reservoir volume balance as $G = \frac{m \cdot N \cdot B_{oi}}{B_{gi}}$.

3 Benchmark Verification: HW2 Question 1 & Question 2

Question 1 (Active Default — Gas-Cap Drive)

With $p_i = 3300\text{ psia}$, $N_p$ in MMSTB, $G_p$ in MMMSCF, and $B_g$ in cuft/SCF ($B_{gi} = 0.00488505\text{ cuft/SCF} = 0.00087006\text{ rb/SCF}$), OLS regression over the 6 producing pressure steps yields $N = 108.68\text{ MMSTB}$, $m = 0.5414$, and $G = 84.60\text{ BSCF}$ ($84,601.4\text{ MMSCF}$) with $R^2 = 0.9677$.

Question 2 (Solution-Gas Drive Check)

With $p_i = 2740\text{ psia}$, $N_p$ in MMSTB, $G_p$ in MMSCF, and $B_g$ in rb/STB, $F / (E_o + E_{fw})$ remains nearly constant around $530.2\text{ MMSTB}$ with a near-zero slope ($m \approx 0$, $G \approx 0$), confirming a volumetric solution-gas drive reservoir with no initial gas cap.
